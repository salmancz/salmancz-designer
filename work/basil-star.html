Portfolio / Logo & Branding / Basil Star
Logo & Brand Identity · Finance & Investment

A trust-led logo and brand identity for Basil Star, a finance firm that wanted to look human, not corporate.

I designed the logo and brand identity for Basil Star, a finance and investment company seeking a mark that signals professionalism and stability without feeling like every other bank. The result is a geometric star lettermark paired with a modern serif and a navy palette, built to scale across print and digital.

01 · The Brief

The brief: credible, but not cold

Basil Star is a finance and investment company that helps individuals and businesses make sound financial decisions. The founder, Shree Hari, wanted an identity that read as professional, stable, and forward-thinking without slipping into the generic, overly corporate look most finance brands settle for. As he put it, the goal was something direct, bold, and human that still communicated the firm's values to both individual clients and business stakeholders.

The practical requirement was a clean, minimal mark that stayed legible and adaptable across every platform, from a favicon to a presentation slide.

02 · Craft & Visual System

The approach: a star that carries a B

I crafted a geometric logo that blends classic finance symbolism with modern elegance. The star stands for guidance, clarity, and excellence, and its pointed shape suggests direction and precision, while the letter B is subtly integrated into the star form. A modern serif typeface adds tradition and seriousness while keeping the brand approachable.

Typography and color were chosen to reinforce trust: a deep navy blue with light neutrals, plus flexible lockups so the identity holds up in different layouts.

03 · Execution & Deliverables

Process and deliverables

The work moved through six phases: discovery and strategy, concept development, typography and color, application mockups, the final design suite, and scalability planning. The mark was tested across business cards, presentation slides, brochures, web headers, favicons, and social icons before sign-off.

The final package included ready-to-use logo files in SVG, PDF, PNG, and EPS, brand guidelines, logo variations, and templates. The modular structure was designed to support future sub-brands and to stay current without frequent redesigns. Tools used were After Effects and Photoshop.
