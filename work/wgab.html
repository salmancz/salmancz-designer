Portfolio / UI/UX / WGAB
App & Product UI/UX · Gamified Quiz App / Entertainment

A roadmap-driven quiz interface built to turn casual players into repeat users.

WGAB is a gamified quiz app that blends entertainment with interactive challenges, progressive rewards, and achievement tracking. I designed the full UI/UX, from wireframes to high-fidelity screens, to drive engagement and long-term retention.

RoleProduct UI/UX Designer
Year2025
DeliverablesUser Research, Wireframing, Figma UI Kit, Prototyping
IndustryGamified Quiz App / Entertainment
01 · The Brief

The challenge

WGAB needed an interface that made a quiz app feel like a game worth returning to. The design had to boost engagement through visually guided gamification, streamline navigation for quiz access and reward tracking, and encourage repeat usage through a structured reward system. Above all, it had to work mobile-first, holding user attention long enough to build lasting retention.

  • User Flows & Wireframes
  • High-Fidelity UI
  • Figma Design System
  • Interactive Prototype
02 · Craft & Visual System

The approach

We ran a user-centric process across six phases: discovery and strategy, UX wireframing, visual design, responsive layouts, a gamification design system, and handoff support. Wireframes mapped the full journey from onboarding to reward claiming, cutting friction while reinforcing a sense of progress. A roadmap-style navigation reflected advancement, and color-coded reward elements, badges, and progress meters carried the gamification through every screen.

03 · Execution & Deliverables

What we delivered

The result is an interactive roadmap system that streamlines challenge navigation and motivates consistent advancement, backed by visual feedback that improves clarity and satisfaction. Thumb-zone-friendly navigation with floating buttons keeps touch interaction consistent across devices. A reusable component library, dynamic badge variants, and dark and light mode tokens made the system easy for developers to integrate and scale, handed off as clickable prototypes and dev-ready Figma assets with animation specs and sprint support.
