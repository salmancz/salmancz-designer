Portfolio / Web Development / Falcon Autotech Virtual Tour
Web UI/UX Design · Warehouse & Intralogistics Automation

An interactive virtual tour of Falcon Autotech's operations, built on a custom plugin engineered for speed.

Falcon Autotech, a warehouse and intralogistics automation company, wanted to showcase its operations as an interactive experience. I crafted a custom WordPress plugin that turns them into a virtual tour, using clickable zones linked to high-quality animated video with compression and lazy loading for fast, responsive performance across every device.

01 · The Brief

The Challenge

Falcon Autotech, a warehouse and intralogistics automation company, wanted to create an interactive digital experience that showcased its operations through a dynamic virtual tour. The tour had to integrate high-quality animated video content while staying fast and responsive across desktops, tablets, and smartphones, with room to add new sectors as the business grows.

  • Web UI/UX Design
  • Visual Hierarchy
  • Responsive System
  • Conversion Layout
02 · Craft & Visual System

The Approach

Rather than lean on third-party platforms, I developed a bespoke WordPress plugin. It pairs a virtual tour of clickable zones, each linked to high-quality animated video, with advanced video compression and lazy loading so high-definition media loads quickly without losing visual clarity. A modular backend setup allows sectors to be added or removed easily, and CMS-level editing means new sectors can be added without development help.

03 · Execution & Deliverables

The Outcome

The finished platform lets visitors explore Falcon Autotech's operational areas interactively through clickable video points, with sub-second video switching and preloading even on mobile data. Built on a custom plugin with cross-browser testing, touch-friendly navigation, and an optimised media architecture, the virtual tour delivers a minimalist, branded, distraction-free experience and a scalable foundation for future additions.
