Portfolio / UI/UX / Learnerly
App & Product UI/UX · Education / EdTech

A gamified, mobile-first learning platform designed to keep EdTech students coming back.

Learnerly is an online education platform offering courses in programming, design, business, and more. I delivered the complete UI/UX design, building a scalable interface that makes course discovery effortless, uses gamification to drive motivation, and works seamlessly across devices.

RoleProduct UI/UX Designer
Year2025
DeliverablesUser Research, Wireframing, Figma UI Kit, Prototyping
IndustryEducation / EdTech
01 · The Brief

The objective

Learnerly needed an interface that could scale with a growing course catalog while keeping learners engaged. The goal was to make course discovery effortless, introduce gamification to sustain motivation, deliver a seamless experience across devices, and sharpen visual clarity for navigation and progress tracking. This was a complete UI/UX engagement for an Education Technology product, not a surface-level refresh.

  • User Flows & Wireframes
  • High-Fidelity UI
  • Figma Design System
  • Interactive Prototype
02 · Craft & Visual System

The approach

The team led with intuitive navigation and a learner-centric dashboard, then layered in gamified elements like badges, streaks, progress bars, and achievement milestones to reward consistent study. Work ran through the full design lifecycle: discovery and user research via surveys and interviews, modular wireframes and user flows, a scalable design system with custom icons and a calm color palette, and mobile-first responsive layouts. Everything was brought to life as high-fidelity Figma prototypes ready for handoff.

03 · Execution & Deliverables

The outcome

Gamification features such as streaks and badges encouraged consistent course activity. The personalized dashboard improved clarity of user progress and the course roadmap, and the mobile-first design increased platform accessibility and daily usage. The result is an EdTech interface built to grow with the product while keeping learners motivated to return.
