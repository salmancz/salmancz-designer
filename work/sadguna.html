Logo & Brand Identity · Jewelry & Sacred Artifacts

A jewelry brand mark that reads as both sacred and premium.

Sadguna is a premium jewelry and divine statue store that needed a visual identity bridging luxury with spirituality. I designed a custom logo and full brand system that merges the letter 'S' with Lord Ganesh, anchored by a gold and emerald green palette across packaging, signage, and digital.

01 · The Brief

The Challenge

Sadguna is a premium jewelry and divine statue store known for its elegant craftsmanship and spiritual sensibility. The brand needed a visual identity that bridges luxury with spirituality, one that felt timeless and elevated across packaging, signage, and digital without leaning too far toward either.

The objective was to design a brand identity that captures the essence of spiritual richness and luxury. That meant combining divinity and elegance in a single mark, merging Lord Ganesh symbolism with brand elements, then developing a color system and typography that could carry across retail, packaging, and digital use.

02 · Craft & Visual System

The Approach

I designed a custom symbol that merges the letter 'S' with Lord Ganesh, representing wisdom, prosperity, and spiritual harmony. The mark was built to feel sacred and sophisticated at the same time, so it reads as premium to jewelry buyers while carrying genuine spiritual meaning.

From there the team developed the supporting color system, typography, and applications, then packaged everything into an identity system ready for retail, packaging, and digital use.

03 · Execution & Deliverables

The Identity System

The palette pairs gold and emerald green to signify wealth, grace, and renewal, balancing opulence with spirituality. Typography uses a classic serif typeface to reflect heritage, tradition, and fine craftsmanship, ensuring the identity aligns with high-end expectations.

Deliverables included logo files in all formats (SVG, EPS, PNG, and PDF) with gold and flat variants, brand guidelines covering spacing, color codes, font usage, and do's and don'ts, application mockups for packaging, signage, and print collateral, and ready-to-use assets for marketing and digital platforms.
