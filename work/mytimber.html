Web UI/UX Design · E-commerce / Furniture & Home Decor

A minimalist furniture store built to sell, and simple enough for the team to run.

I designed the UI/UX, visual hierarchy, and responsive web interface for MyTimber.me, a premium e-commerce store for a Germany-based furniture and home decor retailer. The build pairs a clean, minimalist storefront with Sanity CMS and secure Stripe and PayPal checkout so the team can manage inventory without a developer.

01 · The Brief

The brief

MyTimber.me is a Germany-based retailer of premium furniture and home decor with a minimalist brand aesthetic. They needed an e-commerce platform that matched that look while making shopping smooth and intuitive.

The store had to do three things well: help customers find products through advanced filtering, take payments securely, and let the internal team manage products without waiting on a developer for every change.

  • Web UI/UX Design
  • Visual Hierarchy
  • Responsive System
  • Conversion Layout
02 · Craft & Visual System

What we built

I developed a responsive storefront with a custom frontend in HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, tied to Sanity CMS for content and product management. Checkout runs on Stripe and PayPal, and the site is backed by hosting and ongoing maintenance.

The experience includes dynamic filtering by price and material, a real-time shopping cart, and layouts tuned for every screen size. Because inventory and content live in the CMS, the MyTimber team updates the catalog directly, with no development delays.

03 · Execution & Deliverables

The result

The finished store is fast, lightweight, and built to scale as the catalog grows. Navigation is clean and low-friction, load times are quick on mobile, and payments are secure by default.

As MyTimber CEO Rob Hill put it: "I'm so glad we worked with them, they truly understood what we wanted and delivered it perfectly."
