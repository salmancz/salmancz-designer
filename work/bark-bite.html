Logo & Brand Identity · Pet Food

A pet food identity built to win the shelf and the pet parent's heart.

Bark & Bite is a pet food brand serving dogs, cats, and their owners with fun, care, and premium quality. I designed the logo and full branding system, pairing a bold rounded wordmark with a bright, pet-friendly palette and packaging built to stand out on retail shelves.

RoleBrand & Identity Designer
Year2025
DeliverablesLogo Design, Visual Identity, Brand Guidelines
IndustryPet Food
01 · The Brief

The brief: joy, warmth, and shelf standout

Bark & Bite brings fun, care, and premium quality into the daily lives of dogs, cats, and their owners. The goal was an emotionally resonant brand identity built on a bold, fun wordmark, a bright pet-friendly palette, and packaging that connects with pet parents while differentiating the brand on a crowded retail shelf.

02 · Craft & Visual System

The approach: playful design with retail discipline

We blended joy and clarity through a humorous, friendly wordmark set in a rounded typeface, supported by bold illustrations and cute pet visuals that build an emotional bond. The color system pairs pink, sky blue, purple, and white to signal joy, creativity, trust, and cleanliness. Retail mockups were tested to check shelf visibility before finalizing.

03 · Execution & Deliverables

The result: a brand that stands out

The finished identity captures the warmth and energy of the Bark & Bite name, with eye-catching packaging designed to hold its own in busy aisles. Consistent bright colors and pet-themed illustrations drive recognition, delivered as a full brand pack covering discovery, logo, color and illustration, packaging, and real-world testing.
