Portfolio / Web Development / Copyva - Licensing Platform
Web UI/UX Design · Digital Licensing / Copyright Tech (music & video rights)

A digital licensing platform that connects copyright owners and creators with secure, real-time transactions.

Copyva is a digital licensing platform that connects copyright owners with content creators for secure music and video license transactions. I led the full frontend and backend build on React, Node.js, and AWS.

RoleWeb UI/UX Designer
Year2025
DeliverablesWeb UI/UX Design, Visual Hierarchy, Responsive System
IndustryDigital Licensing / Copyright Tech (music & video rights)
01 · The Brief

The objective

Copyva set out to build a marketplace where copyright owners and content creators could license music and video assets without friction. That meant a platform that stayed intuitive for both rights holders and creators, handled secure transactions in real time across two asset types, and connected a clean frontend to live backend data. Performance and scalability were requirements from day one, not afterthoughts, since the platform had to hold up as content libraries and traffic grew.

02 · Craft & Visual System

The build

I delivered the full stack: frontend development, backend integration, and end-to-end website design and development. The interface was built in React with a mobile-first, responsive layout that simplifies license exploration and rights management, surfacing real-time license availability. REST APIs tie the frontend to the backend so transactions, asset delivery, and account management stay in sync, and real-time syncing keeps license updates, user accounts, and asset uploads current. The platform runs on Node.js and is hosted on AWS with secure authentication flows, keeping it fast and compliant under load.

03 · Execution & Deliverables

The outcome

The launched platform gives users an intuitive, responsive experience that removes friction from browsing and licensing. Its backend supports large-scale content operations with real-time syncing, and the modular AWS architecture leaves room to scale as Copyva grows. In the words of Thomas, CEO of Copyva: I provided proper delivery and continued assistance.
