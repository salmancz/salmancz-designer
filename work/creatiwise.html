Logo & Brand Identity · Web Development & Digital Solutions

A monogram that fuses "C", the infinity symbol, and a red square into one identity for Creatiwise.

I designed the logo and visual identity for Creatiwise, a web development company that thrives on collaboration, communication, and long-term client success. The mark merges a "C" monogram with an infinity symbol and a grounded red square to signal continuity, trust, and strength across digital and print.

01 · The Brief

The Brief

Creatiwise is a forward-thinking web development agency focused on delivering exceptional digital solutions while nurturing long-term client partnerships. They needed a visual identity that reflects their core values of communication, collaboration, and stability while standing out in a competitive market. The goal was a logo that conveys both innovation and trust, aligning with Creatiwise's mission to offer reliable, balanced services.

02 · Craft & Visual System

The Approach

The work opened with brand discovery. We immersed ourselves in Creatiwise's vision as a digital agency built on collaboration, communication, and long-term client success, conducted workshops to define a brand tone that was reliable, clear, and growth-oriented, identified competitor visuals to differentiate through symbolism and simplicity, and aligned on three brand pillars of trust, collaboration, and innovation. From there we merged the letter "C" with an infinity symbol to capture continuity and trust, incorporated a geometric red square for stability, strength, and grounded solutions, and crafted a symmetrical, circular form that feels unified and visually balanced at any scale.

A minimalist black, white, and red palette carried the identity, with black for professionalism, red for energy, and white for space and balance. A clean sans-serif typeface kept the system legible and digital-friendly. We then placed the logo in mockups across desktop, mobile, business stationery, and social media, verifying alignment and balance on document headers and presentation slides.

03 · Execution & Deliverables

The Outcome

The project shipped as a comprehensive logo suite and brand asset pack. Deliverables include the logo in all standard formats (SVG, PNG, EPS, PDF), primary and secondary versions for different layouts, color codes, typography specs, logo usage rules, a brand guideline document, and mockups for key applications across web, stationery, and social media. The color system of black, white, and red ensures legibility and brand recognition across digital screens, business cards, and developer hand-offs, and the logo scales cleanly from websites to pitch decks and business stationery while staying recognizable and true to Creatiwise's values.
