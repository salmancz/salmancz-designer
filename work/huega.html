Brand Starter Pack · Fine Jewelry

Huega, jewels of heritage.

I crafted the Huega premium jewelry brand from the ground up through its Brand Starter Pack, delivering logo design, brand identity, UI/UX, and an e-commerce website. The launch cut bounce rate by 28% and grew product views per session 2.5x.

01 · The Brief

The brief: position Huega as a premium jewelry name

Huega set out to enter the premium jewelry market with a brand that read as luxury and craftsmanship at every touchpoint. The work needed to do two jobs at once, establish a distinctive visual identity and back it with an e-commerce experience that turned browsing into buying. Discovery mapped Huega's positioning, its customers, and the emotional tone the brand had to strike.

  • Brand Identity
  • Logo & Monogram
  • Packaging Design
  • E-Commerce UI/UX
  • Design System
02 · Craft & Visual System

The deliverables: brand identity and an e-commerce store

I delivered logo design, brand identity, UI/UX, and website development as one package. The identity centered on a minimalist, elegant logomark, high-contrast typography, and a curated palette of soft neutrals with gold tones, plus custom assets for packaging, social media, and product labels. The store was designed around the product, with full-width visuals, interactive detail pages with animation, and dark and light modes, all built mobile-first on a CMS backend with schema markup and SEO-ready architecture.

03 · Execution & Deliverables

The result: engagement that moved the numbers

Within the first month after launch, Huega saw a 28% lower bounce rate and 2.5x more product views per session, a clear signal that visitors were staying and exploring. Founder Dorian Fraser: "Our business has seen real growth since partnering with I. Highly Recommend it!"
