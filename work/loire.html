Brand Starter Pack · Luxury Cosmetics

Loire, timeless Parisian beauty across packaging and web.

Loire is a premium cosmetics brand inspired by timeless French elegance. I delivered a complete Brand Starter Pack, pairing a refined visual identity with a conversion-focused e-commerce website that positions Loire as a premium player in beauty.

RoleBrand & Web UI Designer
Year2025
DeliverablesBrand Identity, Cosmetic Packaging, E-Commerce UI
IndustryCosmetics & Luxury Beauty
01 · The Brief

The brief

Loire is a premium cosmetics brand inspired by timeless French elegance. The company needed a distinctive brand identity and a high-performance e-commerce website to showcase its product line and drive customer engagement. The objective was to build a luxury identity and a user-focused shopping experience that elevates product appeal and simplifies the purchase journey, with responsive mobile-first performance and a backend the team could manage on its own.

  • Luxury Wordmark
  • Bottle & Box Mockups
  • Pastel & Gold Palette
  • E-Commerce UI Design
02 · Craft & Visual System

What we built

I delivered a full Brand Starter Pack. That covered the visual identity: a logomark and wordmark, a soft palette of deep purple, dusty rose, and pale lavender, and brand patterns for packaging, print, and digital. On the web side we designed the UX and UI around an intuitive shopping journey, then developed a responsive WordPress and Elementor store with CMS-driven content, product filtering and sorting, and SEO and speed work including image compression and lazy loading. Design was handled in Figma.

03 · Execution & Deliverables

The outcome

The launched platform gave Loire a cohesive premium presence across every touchpoint. Refined visuals, an intuitive UX, and clean branding raised credibility and supported engagement across devices. Optimized product pages made browsing and buying effortless, while a modular backend lets the team update content without developer dependency. I followed launch with brand assets, training, and ongoing support.
