Portfolio / UI/UX / MA Auto Cars
App & Product UI/UX · Automotive (online car & bike servicing)

A vehicle servicing app that turns booking a mechanic into a single tap.

MA Auto Cars is a vehicle service platform that connects car and bike owners with verified local mechanics for scheduled or emergency repairs. I designed a mobile-first UI/UX built around fast booking, real-time technician tracking, and light and dark mode accessibility.

RoleProduct UI/UX Designer
Year2025
DeliverablesUser Research, Wireframing, Figma UI Kit, Prototyping
IndustryAutomotive (online car & bike servicing)
01 · The Brief

The brief: make vehicle servicing feel effortless

MA Auto Cars connects users with verified local mechanics for everything from oil changes to doorstep servicing, covering both scheduled maintenance and emergency repairs. The goal for this automotive UI/UX project was a modern, user-centric interface that simplified booking, enabled real-time tracking, and stayed clear and legible for people using the app on the move.

Discovery confirmed three things that shaped every decision: users prefer quick, tap-based booking, real-time visibility of a technician builds confidence, and dark mode meaningfully improves usability in low-light and roadside scenarios.

02 · Craft & Visual System

Designing for speed, clarity, and trust

The interface was structured around effortless booking flows with one-tap service selection, clear dashboards, and discoverable calls to action. A clean bottom navigation with thumb-friendly controls keeps core actions within reach, while dashboard widgets and smart alerts surface what matters. Every screen ships in both light and dark mode.

Live tracking is central to the experience. GPS-enabled technician tracking, animated status updates, and visual confirmation cues, backed by sound and vibration alerts, keep users informed from booking to arrival. These microinteractions were designed to build trust and cut the need to contact support.

03 · Execution & Deliverables

Built for every screen and every scenario

The design was developed for fast load times and device compatibility across Android and iOS, using modular components and offline-safe functionality so the app holds up in real-world conditions. A mobile-first layout keeps the experience driver-friendly on small screens.

Post-launch work included usability testing, hosting setup, admin walkthroughs, performance monitoring, and feedback channels. The result is a simplified booking flow that reduces drop-off, live tracking that lowers support inquiries, and an improved visual hierarchy that makes features easier to find.
