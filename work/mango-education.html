Portfolio / Web Development / Mango Education
Web UI/UX Design · Education

A mobile-first learning platform that makes Mango Education's programs easy to find and trust.

I designed the UI/UX, visual hierarchy, and responsive web interface for a responsive, SEO-focused website for Mango Education, an experiential learning platform that partners with schools and government initiatives to build scientific thinking in children. The site organizes programs, events, and stories into a clear, CMS-managed experience for parents, educators, and students.

RoleWeb UI/UX Designer
Year2025
DeliverablesWeb UI/UX Design, Visual Hierarchy, Responsive System
IndustryEducation
01 · The Brief

The brief

Mango Education runs hands-on, experiential learning programs and partners with schools and government initiatives to grow curiosity and scientific thinking in children. It needed a digital home that reflected that mission and gave three distinct audiences, parents, educators, and students, a clear path to its offerings. The old experience made programs hard to browse and did not hold up well across devices, so the work centered on structure, speed, and a design that felt both credible and playful.

  • Web UI/UX Design
  • Visual Hierarchy
  • Responsive System
  • Conversion Layout
02 · Craft & Visual System

What I crafted

The web development approach was strategy-first and mobile-first. We mapped user journeys, segmented content for each audience, and wireframed a structured layout for the key programs before design. The build used performance-first code, custom reusable modules, and a CMS so the Mango team can add programs, events, and stories without a developer. SEO was baked into the architecture with keyword-rich structure, technical enhancements, and mobile-first audits.

03 · Execution & Deliverables

The result

The launched site pairs a vibrant, youthful design with clean, credible structure that reassures parents and educators. Improved UX and performance increased average session duration, the SEO-friendly build boosted visibility for education-related searches, and the CMS gives the team the flexibility to keep content current. Mobile-first responsiveness means the experience holds across phones, tablets, and desktops.
