05 · About

Design that earns trust before a word is read.

I am Salman, a Graphic, Brand Identity, and UI/UX Designer based in Coimbatore, India. Over the past three years across agency and product work, I have designed more than 40 brand identities, packaging systems, mobile apps, and conversion-focused websites for startups and established businesses across the US, UK, Europe, and India.

My work sits at the intersection of brand storytelling and interface precision. I enjoy the craft of distilling a company's positioning into a single timeless mark, testing it on bottle labels, velvet ring boxes, and app icons, and then carrying that exact visual rhythm into a Figma design system and live website.

Because I also understand front-end code (HTML, CSS, Tailwind, React) and user analytics (Hotjar, GA4), my interfaces are built for real-world conversion and developer handoff, lifting client lead conversion by 60% and user retention by 35%.
